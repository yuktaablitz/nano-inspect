When does a part go to a human in the cloud?

After both edge models have looked at a part, it lands in one evidence bucket. We measured how often each bucket happens for defective and for good parts on held-out data. With your line's defect rate, Bayes' rule gives the probability that a part in that bucket is defective. A part is escalated only if the cheaper automatic decision still costs more than a human review.
